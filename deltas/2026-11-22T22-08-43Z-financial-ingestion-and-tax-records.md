# Docket — financial ingestion and tax records

**Reviewed proposal** · November 22, 2026 · [SPEC 1.2](../SPEC.md), implementation `599c7d7c43d2`, native contract v69. The [invoice numbering and artifact proposal](2026-11-22T21-38-05Z-invoice-materialization-boundary.md) remains pending and can be reviewed or shipped independently.

## 1. Outcome and first release

“Find the receipt for this card charge, tell me who paid, and show what still needs review for taxes” should lead back to one economic expense and its evidence. F1 can ship on **one authorized funding account and one manual or CSV statement period**. The first combined F1/F2 milestone adds one explicitly selected billing source and reconciles receipts to that period. Accept either source first. Once linked, both support the same cost. A direct bank connection, external invoice renderer and tax engine are unnecessary for that milestone.

## 3. One purchase, several kinds of evidence

| Record | Meaning and identity |
| --- | --- |
| Funding account and payment instrument | Identify the account, institution, currency where known, liable party and the card or payment method used. Record optional issuer, masked suffix, cardholder, authorized-user role and effective dates using existing Entity refs. An account may have several cards or none. Replacement cards preserve history. Last four digits are a display hint and can collide. |
| Account activity observation | Retain the selected account, source, external row ID if available, statement/row provenance, original currency and amount, debit/credit direction relative to that account, transaction/posting dates, pending/posted/reversed state and description. Distinguish a pending authorization from a posted charge and retain revisions when a source corrects it. |
| Expense and evidence | Keep `Expense` as the canonical cost. Attach dated, attributable links from email, receipt, invoice, statement row or manual report to its exact revision or intake candidate. Track which facts each source supports and whether protected bytes can actually be retrieved. A new evidence link or attachment copy creates no new cost. |
| Reconciliation link | Bind exact quantities between observations, expenses, transfers and existing settlement refs. Support split tender, multiple purchases on one charge, partial refunds and one reimbursement covering several costs. Bound allocations per source, economic purpose and currency. Preserve unresolved remainders. Opposite legs of a card payment or transfer can describe one movement. |
| Activity allocation and tax treatment | Allocate the operator's economic share across personal, employee, contracting and other activities with amounts/percentages, rationale and a remainder. Keep tax-year/jurisdiction-specific classification and review separately revisioned. Funding, expense ownership, project benefit and tax treatment can all differ. |
| Recurring expectation | A confirmed vendor/account schedule records cadence, amount range, currency, next window and active/cancelled dates. Compare it with observed charges. Missing, doubled or materially changed charges create a suppressible exception. A forecast never posts an expense. |

**Shared-card example.** An authorized user buys a USD 120 subscription on a parent's card, then pays the parent USD 120. F1 records the purchase, card and liable party, the reported user/beneficiary, and the outgoing repayment classified as a transfer. The transfer can remain unmatched. F3 can associate that repayment with the purchase and pair the movement with its other leg when authorized evidence exists. A card-bill payment settles account liability separately. The expense report still shows USD 120. If there is evidence for a proposed 70% business allocation, the workpaper shows USD 84 proposed and USD 36 personal, with rationale and review status. Paying back the cardholder does not establish a deduction. Enrollment of the operator's instrument conveys no permission to ingest the rest of the household's spending.

**Travel example.** A contractor incurs USD 300, has USD 250 approved and later receives USD 200. Use the existing expense, approval and applied-payment records. Show USD 50 outstanding on the approved claim, USD 50 outside that approval, and a separate open question about tax treatment. A later merchant refund or employer reimbursement retains its own date/source and reopens affected reconciliation. Preserve prior reports and any issued invoice.

## 4. Owned-mail intake and local inference

**Mail routing is a separate decision.** Two plausible routes are an authorized intake client reading a selected mailbox/label and submitting evidence, or a scoped reader over Docket's existing Gmail observations with separate protected retrieval of selected parts. Any route must establish the actual receiving account and admission rule *before* exposing content to financial extraction. The visible `To` or `From` header, forwarded text and a model's mailbox guess cannot establish that a message belongs to the authorized stream. An alias sharing an inbox needs a verified delivery mapping or an explicitly authorized label. Keep the scope unresolved for Bcc, forwarding or delegation where trustworthy evidence is missing. This proposal selects neither transport and enables no inbox.

Whichever route is chosen, persist a revisioned, revocable intake policy with owner, authenticated account, admitted label/verified alias and optional sender filters, start/backfill boundary, evidence retention and allowed actions. Use bounded checkpoints and idempotent retries. Check current policy and grants again before retention or adoption. Disabling a source stops queued acquisition and promotion under that policy while preserving genuine prior evidence. Attachments, sender names, message text and model output grant no authority. No mail-label changes or email replies are needed.

**Aphelion is an engineering reference only.** Its extraction pipeline demonstrates deterministic parsing first, bounded model calls, grounding fields in captured evidence and retaining partial results after failure. Financial intake owns its schemas, fixtures and recovery. It must run independently of Aphelion modules, infrastructure and extraction contracts. The local Ollama runtime reported `nimble:9b-q8_0` and `qwen3.5:9b-q4_K_M` loaded during drafting. These are configurable client choices. Model names and endpoints stay outside Docket's canonical contracts. [Aphelion SPEC §8](https://github.com/nkuhanas/aphelion/blob/fccdd10a7620cb8ea9acbfbd10429b576d7de226/SPEC.md#8-job-ingestion-url-only-and-server-owned) · [Aphelion inference tests](https://github.com/nkuhanas/aphelion/blob/fccdd10a7620cb8ea9acbfbd10429b576d7de226/tests/unit/test_inference.py)

1. Capture evidence under the selected policy. Parse CSV and machine-readable amounts/dates deterministically. An optional local client can use **Nimble for bounded classification** (bill, receipt, statement, other or uncertain) and **Qwen for structured extraction** from admitted text. Either stage can be skipped. Bound concurrency at the selected inference runtime. A negative classifier decision is recoverable. No model is needed to record an expense or import a valid statement.
2. For each proposed merchant, invoice ID/date, billing period, currency, tax/total or masked instrument hint, retain source and page/part/row/span, extraction method and uncertainty. Validate normalized decimals and dates against source text. Distinguish subtotal, total due, past balance and amount paid. Keep image-only or unreadable receipts with an evidence gap. Any later OCR produces a separately attributed derivative.
3. The client owns inference, scheduling and bounded retries. Record actual parser/model/version, prompt/schema revision, outcome and source anchors. Bad evidence, model timeout or unavailable weights leave a partial candidate. No silent model substitution or remote forwarding of financial documents. Reuse protected source storage and Docket's request/ChangeSet path. The inference worker receives no independent financial authority.
4. Default automation to capture, propose and suggest matches. A separately authorized writer may adopt the candidate or link it to an existing expense. A future standing rule may narrowly admit deterministic, unambiguous matches within the chosen account. A billing email alone cannot establish a posted charge, receipt of money, business-use fraction or reviewed tax classification. A review queue should ask once per material conflict and leave ordinary work available.

## 5. Statement intake and reconciliation

F1 assigns stable identities to manual and imported activity. An explicit transfer classification on a single row keeps a repayment or card payment out of expense totals even when its counterpart is unknown. F2 can attach billing evidence to an observed purchase. Pairing transfers across accounts, splitting amounts and matching refunds or reimbursements are F3 operations on the same F1 identities. Preview the account, date convention, sign convention, currency, masked instrument, covered period and row counts before committing an unfamiliar mapping. Reject ambiguous dates, missing currency or contradictory debit/credit columns on the affected rows. Preserve the original source, row positions and import disposition under the retention policy. Allow a bounded batch to resume from a durable receipt. Record opening/closing balances and reconcile them **when supplied**. Otherwise report partial or unknown statement coverage.

Use the account-scoped provider transaction ID when present. Replay of the same statement/row is idempotent. Different files with overlapping periods, duplicate same-day amounts, changing pending IDs, tips and changed posting dates need explicit linkage or review. Reordered files and forwarded receipts must not generate a second economic cost merely because a file or message ID changed. A content checksum identifies bytes. It cannot uniquely identify two legitimate equal purchases. Confirm transfer legs, repayments, refunds, disputes and FX/fees with explicit source evidence. Preserve both currencies and observed amounts for FX. Never net different currencies using an inferred rate.

Track billed, observed pending, posted, matched, reimbursable, approved, received and refunded as distinct facts. For an incoming reimbursement, link or record **one** existing-style payment and apply it to the claim. Preserve an unmatched deposit if its purpose is unknown. Correct links/allocations through versioned reversals. Use one database transaction for an adopted expense/payment and its source match, with owner checks, stable operation IDs, expected versions and PostgreSQL quantity constraints. Competing workers must not create duplicate payments or consume the same amount twice.

## 6. Tax-year workpapers and evidence export

A workpaper records tax year, jurisdiction, activity/engagement, exact source expense revision, ownership share, business/personal split, purpose, proposed category, known refunds and reimbursements, evidence refs, reviewer, review date and status. Distinguish unreviewed, needs evidence, reviewed for inclusion and excluded. Keep assets requiring basis/depreciation review, employment-related costs, contractor costs, travel/vehicle details and uncertain treatment visible separately. Proposed business use and reviewed deductibility have separate amounts, evidence and status. A reviewer can accept, revise or exclude any portion. A reimbursement records a financial fact and flags affected treatments for review. Only an explicit, revisioned treatment decision changes the reviewed deduction. Retain the earlier treatment, expense and rationale.

For U.S. workpapers, support the facts a preparer may need across multiple records, including payee, amount, proof of payment, date, description and business purpose. Travel and vehicle entries can need trip-specific facts such as destination or business mileage. Treat these as prompts for evidence. Record year-specific guidance/basis for a treatment rather than hardcoding a percentage or carrying a prior-year decision forward. [IRS business recordkeeping guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/what-kind-of-records-should-i-keep) · [IRS Publication 463, chapter 5, table 5-1](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p463)

**How much tax have I already paid?** Reuse F1 account activity for estimated payments, balance payments and tax refunds. Link withholding to a supported payroll statement, annual form or existing settlement deduction. A net wage deposit alone supplies no withholding amount. Store the tax classification as a workpaper allocation against the exact source, using tax authority/jurisdiction, tax type, designated tax year, payment or withholding date, amount/currency, direction, evidence and review status. One movement can be split across documented years or jurisdictions up to its amount. Unknown designations stay unallocated. This uses the existing observation, settlement and workpaper identities without a second tax-payment ledger or a new money-movement tool.

Report documented withholding, estimated payments, other direct tax payments and refunds received in separate columns by year, jurisdiction, tax type and currency. Show gross payments/withholding and net after refunds, plus pending, reversed or unallocated amounts separately. Preserve posting date and designated year independently. Count a posted debit and its tax confirmation once. A pay stub, its year-to-date figure, an annual form and a linked settlement deduction may describe the same withheld amount. Reconcile overlapping evidence before counting it. These totals answer a recordkeeping question. Computing tax liability or initiating payment remains outside scope.

Export year/activity/currency totals, reviewed and proposed amounts in separate columns, confirmed reimbursements/refunds, unresolved totals and a permission-checked evidence index. Mark missing, metadata-only, expired or inaccessible originals. Let the operator explicitly include authorized originals in a protected export. Pin report membership to exact expense, split, account activity, settlement, treatment and evidence revisions. A late reimbursement, refund, corrected form or source correction flags the affected year and produces a new report basis. The previously exported report remains reproducible, subject to disclosed evidence retention. F5 adds income/withholding reconciliation against authorized pay statements and W-2/1099 evidence. Receiving an annual form does not create another deposit or settle gross wages against a net payment.

## 7. Delivery phases and acceptance

**Review disposition.** The review endorses F1 for implementation on one authorized account with manual/CSV intake. F2 policy and evidence processing can proceed on synthetic and isolated sources independently of the live mailbox decision. The review endorses F3 reconciliation once F1 activity identities exist. F4–F5 have directional approval subject to the tax-payment, withholding and reimbursement cases above before schema finalization. The invoice proposal remains separate.

| Phase | What the operator can do | Acceptance on synthetic records |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **F1 · Funding and statements** | “Import this card statement and show unmatched charges.” Register masked accounts/instruments, retain row identities and classify an explicit transfer. | Replaying overlapping CSVs or a pending-to-posted correction preserves row lineage and two legitimate identical purchases. Cards sharing a suffix stay distinct. A USD 120 purchase and USD 120 repayment produce one USD 120 expense and one unmatched transfer, with no second account or mail source required. |
| **F2 · Selected bills and matching** | “Collect receipts from my selected billing source and match them to the statement.” Designed with F1. Protected file intake can be exercised while routing is undecided. Live acquisition requires verified receiving-account identity, authorized alias or label routing, and approved retention/access. Exercise the intake policy and evidence processing with isolated sources until all three are established. | Email-first and statement-first converge on one cost. Forwarded copies, inaccessible PDFs, wrong-account aliases, injected instructions, missing amount/currency, model failure, revoked policies and concurrent adoption have bounded, attributable outcomes. Run synthetic bills with inference disabled and with a configurable extractor. Keep source evidence and unknown fields usable on model failure. |
| **F3 · Reconciliation and recurrence** | “What am I owed? Which renewal changed price?” Match reimbursements/refunds/transfers and monitor confirmed repeating charges through existing attention preferences. | Start from F1's USD 120 purchase and unmatched transfer. Pair the repayment with an authorized counterpart, then replay. Expense count and total stay one and USD 120. Also reconcile partial reimbursements/refunds and split tender per currency. A missing renewal raises at most one exception per period with known statement coverage. It books no forecast charge. |
| **F4 · Reviewed tax records** | “What have I paid in tax for this year and jurisdiction, and which costs still need review?” | For one year/jurisdiction, USD 600 of documented estimated payments plus USD 400 withheld gives USD 1,000 gross. A USD 100 tax refund gives USD 900 net. A January debit designated for the prior tax year stays there. A second jurisdiction and unknown-year amounts remain separate. A reimbursement flags a mixed-use treatment for review. Its approved amount changes only through a reviewed revision. Test employee and asset cases, split limits and reproducible prior exports. |
| **F5 · Annual reconciliation** | “Compare my annual forms with posted receipts, compensation and withholding.” | A form corroborating the same USD 400 withheld adds zero to the total. A corrected form preserves both versions and exposes any difference for reconciliation. Unexplained differences and incomplete account/form coverage remain explicit. No automatic filing, money movement or direct banking integration. |

**Native contracts.** Extend expense reads and financial context with linked funding, evidence, coverage and exceptions. Return only relevant refs/versions in context. Reuse expense and payment writes for economic events. F1/F2 need small named operations for funding setup, importing an owned statement source, recording manual activity and linking/adopting a candidate. Derive the allocation and evidence records internally. Import by source ref and return counts plus paginated exceptions, rather than putting CSV rows, email bodies or files in tool arguments. F4 needs a scoped treatment write and a year-workpaper read/export. Choose final tool boundaries per phase after measuring actual MCP definitions against SPEC's 4 KiB input, 6 KiB tool definition and 16 KiB normal response limits.

**Storage, privacy and rollout.** Add owner-scoped revisioned funding, observations, links, intake policy and workpapers through ordered migrations. Reuse `Source`, existing protected blobs where suitable, Entities, Engagements, Expense/Payment and immutable financial claims. Backfill no inferred card, alias, split, reviewer or tax treatment. Preserve all historical refs, reports and balances. Keep PANs, CVVs, bank credentials and full account/tax identifiers out of structured fields, logs, notifications and model inputs. Restrict raw evidence access and retention separately from ordinary metadata reads, including shared-account and payroll documents. Rehearse migrations, restart, replay and concurrent matching on isolated PostgreSQL. Test a restored report and evidence permissions before enabling real intake.

**Decisions before a live pilot.** Select the owned-mail route and prove its receiving account/alias or label boundary. Select one authorized statement format and instrument, permitted shared-account rows, backfill window and original-file retention/access. Decide whether any exact matches may auto-link, with candidate-only as the starting setting. U.S. federal reviewable workpapers are the proposed F4 starting scope. Other jurisdictions and tax filing require separate decisions. None of these choices blocks drafting, isolated F1 work or manual records.
