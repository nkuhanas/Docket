# Docket — financial ingestion and tax records

**Proposed for Operator review** · November 22, 2026 · [SPEC 1.2](../SPEC.md), implementation `599c7d7c43d2`, native contract v69. The [invoice numbering and artifact proposal](2026-11-22T21-38-05Z-invoice-materialization-boundary.md) remains pending and can be reviewed or shipped independently.

## 1. Outcome and first release

“Find the receipt for this card charge, tell me who paid, and show what still needs review for taxes” should lead back to one economic expense and its evidence. Start with **one explicitly selected billing source, one funding account and manual or CSV statement intake**. Accept a bill before its charge appears, or a statement before its receipt arrives. Once linked, both support the same cost. The first deliverable is a repeatable reconciliation of one statement period. A direct bank connection, external invoice renderer and tax engine are unnecessary for that milestone.

## 3. One purchase, several kinds of evidence

| Record | Meaning and identity |
| --- | --- |
| Funding account and payment instrument | Identify the account, institution, currency where known, liable party and the card or payment method used. Record optional issuer, masked suffix, cardholder, authorized-user role and effective dates using existing Entity refs. An account may have several cards or none. Replacement cards preserve history. Last four digits are a display hint and can collide. |
| Account activity observation | Retain the selected account, source, external row ID if available, statement/row provenance, original currency and amount, debit/credit direction relative to that account, transaction/posting dates, pending/posted/reversed state and description. Distinguish a pending authorization from a posted charge and retain revisions when a source corrects it. |
| Expense and evidence | Keep `Expense` as the canonical cost. Attach dated, attributable links from email, receipt, invoice, statement row or manual report to its exact revision or intake candidate. Track which facts each source supports and whether protected bytes can actually be retrieved. A new evidence link or attachment copy creates no new cost. |
| Reconciliation link | Bind exact quantities between observations, expenses, transfers and existing settlement refs. Support split tender, multiple purchases on one charge, partial refunds and one reimbursement covering several costs. Bound allocations per source, economic purpose and currency. Preserve unresolved remainders. Opposite legs of a card payment or transfer can describe one movement. |
| Activity allocation and tax treatment | Allocate the operator's economic share across personal, employee, contracting and other activities with amounts/percentages, rationale and a remainder. Keep tax-year/jurisdiction-specific classification and review separately revisioned. Funding, expense ownership, project benefit and tax treatment can all differ. |
| Recurring expectation | A confirmed vendor/account schedule records cadence, amount range, currency, next window and active/cancelled dates. Compare it with observed charges. Missing, doubled or materially changed charges create a suppressible exception. A forecast never posts an expense. |

**Shared-card example.** An authorized user buys a USD 120 subscription on a parent's card, then pays the parent USD 120. Record one purchase, the card and liable party, the reported user/beneficiary, and the repayment as a transfer linked to that purchase. A card-bill payment is another settlement of account liability. The expense report still shows USD 120. If there is evidence for a proposed 70% business allocation, the workpaper shows USD 84 proposed and USD 36 personal, with rationale and review status. Paying back the cardholder does not establish a deduction. Enrollment of the operator's instrument conveys no permission to ingest the rest of the household's spending.

**Travel example.** A contractor incurs USD 300, has USD 250 approved and later receives USD 200. Use the existing expense, approval and applied-payment records. Show USD 50 outstanding on the approved claim, USD 50 outside that approval, and a separate open question about tax treatment. A later merchant refund or employer reimbursement retains its own date/source and reopens affected reconciliation. Preserve prior reports and any issued invoice.

## 4. Owned-mail intake and local inference

**Mail routing is a separate decision.** Two plausible routes are an authorized intake client reading a selected mailbox/label and submitting evidence, or a scoped reader over Docket's existing Gmail observations with separate protected retrieval of selected parts. Any route must establish the actual receiving account and admission rule *before* exposing content to financial extraction. The visible `To` or `From` header, forwarded text and a model's mailbox guess cannot establish that a message belongs to the authorized stream. An alias sharing an inbox needs a verified delivery mapping or an explicitly authorized label. Keep the scope unresolved for Bcc, forwarding or delegation where trustworthy evidence is missing. This proposal selects neither transport and enables no inbox.

Whichever route is chosen, persist a revisioned, revocable intake policy with owner, authenticated account, admitted label/verified alias and optional sender filters, start/backfill boundary, evidence retention and allowed actions. Use bounded checkpoints and idempotent retries. Check current policy and grants again before retention or adoption. Disabling a source stops queued acquisition and promotion under that policy while preserving genuine prior evidence. Attachments, sender names, message text and model output grant no authority. No mail-label changes or email replies are needed.

**Adapt Aphelion's extraction process.** Aphelion registers the source before model work, extracts deterministic facts first, pins the parser/model configuration, validates proposed fields against the captured source, and keeps usable evidence if inference fails. Its Qwen extractor permits one bounded repair and rejects unsupported values. The shared Ollama runtime reported both `qwen3.5:9b-q4_K_M` and `nimble:9b-q8_0` loaded during drafting. This confirms availability at inspection time. Financial extraction quality needs its own fixtures. [Aphelion SPEC §8](https://github.com/nkuhanas/aphelion/blob/fccdd10a7620cb8ea9acbfbd10429b576d7de226/SPEC.md#8-job-ingestion-url-only-and-server-owned) · [Aphelion inference tests](https://github.com/nkuhanas/aphelion/blob/fccdd10a7620cb8ea9acbfbd10429b576d7de226/tests/unit/test_inference.py)

1. Capture evidence under the selected policy. Parse CSV and machine-readable amounts/dates deterministically. An optional local client can use **Nimble for bounded classification** (bill, receipt, statement, other or uncertain) and **Qwen for structured extraction** from admitted text. Either stage can be skipped. Use a queue limit shared with other local workloads. A negative classifier decision is recoverable. No model is needed to record an expense or import a valid statement.
2. For each proposed merchant, invoice ID/date, billing period, currency, tax/total or masked instrument hint, retain source and page/part/row/span, extraction method and uncertainty. Validate normalized decimals and dates against source text. Distinguish subtotal, total due, past balance and amount paid. Keep image-only or unreadable receipts with an evidence gap. Any later OCR produces a separately attributed derivative.
3. The client owns inference, scheduling and bounded retries. Record actual parser/model/version, prompt/schema revision, outcome and source anchors. Bad evidence, model timeout or unavailable weights leave a partial candidate. No silent model substitution or remote forwarding of financial documents. Reuse protected source storage and Docket's request/ChangeSet path. The inference worker receives no independent financial authority.
4. Default automation to capture, propose and suggest matches. A separately authorized writer may adopt the candidate or link it to an existing expense. A future standing rule may narrowly admit deterministic, unambiguous matches within the chosen account. A billing email alone cannot establish a posted charge, receipt of money, business-use fraction or reviewed tax classification. A review queue should ask once per material conflict and leave ordinary work available.

## 5. Statement intake and reconciliation

Manual activity entry and CSV from a selected account are sufficient for F1. Preview the account, date convention, sign convention, currency, masked instrument, covered period and row counts before committing an unfamiliar mapping. Reject ambiguous dates, missing currency or contradictory debit/credit columns on the affected rows. Preserve the original source, row positions and import disposition under the retention policy. Allow a bounded batch to resume from a durable receipt. Record opening/closing balances and reconcile them **when supplied**. Otherwise report partial or unknown statement coverage.

Use the account-scoped provider transaction ID when present. Replay of the same statement/row is idempotent. Different files with overlapping periods, duplicate same-day amounts, changing pending IDs, tips and changed posting dates need explicit linkage or review. Reordered files and forwarded receipts must not generate a second economic cost merely because a file or message ID changed. A content checksum identifies bytes. It cannot uniquely identify two legitimate equal purchases. Confirm transfer legs, repayments, refunds, disputes and FX/fees with explicit source evidence. Preserve both currencies and observed amounts for FX. Never net different currencies using an inferred rate.

Track billed, observed pending, posted, matched, reimbursable, approved, received and refunded as distinct facts. For an incoming reimbursement, link or record **one** existing-style payment and apply it to the claim. Preserve an unmatched deposit if its purpose is unknown. Correct links/allocations through versioned reversals. Use one database transaction for an adopted expense/payment and its source match, with owner checks, stable operation IDs, expected versions and PostgreSQL quantity constraints. Competing workers must not create duplicate payments or consume the same amount twice.

## 6. Tax-year workpapers and evidence export

A workpaper records tax year, jurisdiction, activity/engagement, exact source expense revision, ownership share, business/personal split, purpose, proposed category, known refunds and reimbursements, evidence refs, reviewer, review date and status. Distinguish unreviewed, needs evidence, reviewed for inclusion and excluded. Keep assets requiring basis/depreciation review, employment-related costs, contractor costs, travel/vehicle details and uncertain treatment visible separately. Proposed business use is an attributed assertion. Docket calculates the split using exact money, preserves remainders, and applies only a recorded reviewed treatment to the reviewed subtotal. It does not choose tax eligibility from employer, card or vendor names.

For U.S. workpapers, support the facts a preparer may need across multiple records, including payee, amount, proof of payment, date, description and business purpose. Travel and vehicle entries can need trip-specific facts such as destination or business mileage. Treat these as prompts for evidence. Record year-specific guidance/basis for a treatment rather than hardcoding a percentage or carrying a prior-year decision forward. [IRS business recordkeeping guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/what-kind-of-records-should-i-keep) · [IRS Publication 463, chapter 5, table 5-1](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p463)

Export year/activity/currency totals, reviewed and proposed amounts in separate columns, confirmed reimbursements/refunds, unresolved totals and a permission-checked evidence index. Mark missing, metadata-only, expired or inaccessible originals. Let the operator explicitly include authorized originals in a protected export. Pin report membership to exact expense, split, settlement, treatment and evidence revisions. A late reimbursement, refund, corrected form or source correction flags the affected year and produces a new report basis. The previously exported report remains reproducible, subject to disclosed evidence retention. F5 adds income/withholding reconciliation against authorized pay statements and W-2/1099 evidence. Receiving an annual form does not create another deposit or settle gross wages against a net payment.

## 7. Delivery phases and acceptance

| Phase | What the operator can do | Acceptance on synthetic records |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **F1 · Funding and statements** | “Import this card statement and show unmatched charges.” Register masked accounts/instruments, manual rows or protected CSV, with coverage. | Two cards with the same suffix stay distinct. Overlapping CSVs, a replay, two identical purchases and pending-to-posted correction preserve the right counts. The shared-card repayment creates one expense. |
| **F2 · Selected bills and matching** | “Collect receipts from my selected billing source and match them to the statement.” Designed with F1. Protected file intake can be exercised while routing is undecided. Calling automated mail intake complete requires one verified, explicitly selected route. | Email-first and statement-first converge on one cost. Forwarded copies, inaccessible PDFs, wrong-account aliases, injected instructions, missing amount/currency, model failure, revoked policies and concurrent adoption have bounded, attributable outcomes. Demonstrate the two-model option on representative synthetic bills separately from manual/deterministic intake. |
| **F3 · Reconciliation and recurrence** | “What am I owed? Which renewal changed price?” Match reimbursements/refunds/transfers and monitor confirmed repeating charges through existing attention preferences. | Partial reimbursements, partial refunds, a card payment, split tender and missing evidence reconcile per currency. A missing renewal raises at most one exception per period with known statement coverage. It books no forecast charge. |
| **F4 · Reviewed tax records** | “Export this year's contractor workpapers and show what's still unreviewed.” | A mixed-use purchase, employee cost, asset and cross-year refund preserve attribution, revisions and reviewer basis. Splits cannot exceed their cost, tax subtotals cannot double-count a reimbursed share, and the export reproduces its pinned figures with evidence gaps visible. |
| **F5 · Annual reconciliation** | “Compare my annual forms with posted receipts, compensation and withholding.” | Duplicate and corrected forms preserve both versions. Unexplained differences and incomplete account/form coverage remain explicit. No automatic filing, money movement or direct banking integration. |

**Native contracts.** Extend expense reads and financial context with linked funding, evidence, coverage and exceptions. Return only relevant refs/versions in context. Reuse expense and payment writes for economic events. F1/F2 need small named operations for funding setup, importing an owned statement source, recording manual activity and linking/adopting a candidate. Derive the allocation and evidence records internally. Import by source ref and return counts plus paginated exceptions, rather than putting CSV rows, email bodies or files in tool arguments. F4 needs a scoped treatment write and a year-workpaper read/export. Choose final tool boundaries per phase after measuring actual MCP definitions against SPEC's 4 KiB input, 6 KiB tool definition and 16 KiB normal response limits.

**Storage, privacy and rollout.** Add owner-scoped revisioned funding, observations, links, intake policy and workpapers through ordered migrations. Reuse `Source`, existing protected blobs where suitable, Entities, Engagements, Expense/Payment and immutable financial claims. Backfill no inferred card, alias, split, reviewer or tax treatment. Preserve all historical refs, reports and balances. Keep PANs, CVVs, bank credentials and full account/tax identifiers out of structured fields, logs, notifications and model inputs. Restrict raw evidence access and retention separately from ordinary metadata reads, including shared-account and payroll documents. Rehearse migrations, restart, replay and concurrent matching on isolated PostgreSQL. Test a restored report and evidence permissions before enabling real intake.

**Decisions before a live pilot.** Select the owned-mail route and prove its receiving account/alias or label boundary. Select one authorized statement format and instrument, permitted shared-account rows, backfill window and original-file retention/access. Decide whether any exact matches may auto-link, with candidate-only as the starting setting. U.S. federal reviewable workpapers are the proposed F4 starting scope. Other jurisdictions and tax filing require separate decisions. None of these choices blocks drafting, isolated F1 work or manual records.
